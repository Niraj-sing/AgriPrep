Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how AgriPrep collects, uses, shares, protects and otherwise processes your personal information when you use the AgriPrep website, application and related services.

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how AgriPrep ("AgriPrep", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, shares, protects and otherwise processes your information and personal data through our website and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

The AgriPrep Platform is intended for users in India. Your personal data may be stored and processed in India and may also be processed by third-party service providers used by AgriPrep, where applicable.

By visiting or using the Platform, providing your information, creating an account or using any service offered by AgriPrep, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection and processing of your information as described below.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use or access the Platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information when you create, access or use your AgriPrep account, services and features, or otherwise interact with us.

Depending on the features you use, the information we may collect can include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number, where provided
  • College or university information
  • Degree or field of study
  • Current academic year or semester
  • Target examination information
  • Account and authentication information
  • Profile information that you choose to provide
  • Information relating to your use of AgriPrep features and services
  • Information relating to transactions or purchases made through applicable AgriPrep services

You may choose not to provide certain information. However, refusing to provide information required for a particular feature may prevent you from using that feature.

2. Information Received Through Third-Party Services

AgriPrep uses third-party services to support certain Platform functions.

For example, AgriPrep uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication and account-related functionality.

When you use a third-party service in connection with AgriPrep, information may be processed by that third party in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing information through them.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for purposes necessary to operate, maintain and improve AgriPrep and to provide the services you request.

Depending on the circumstances, we may use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your AgriPrep account
  • Authenticate your account
  • Verify your email address
  • Maintain and display your profile
  • Provide educational and agriculture-related features
  • Process applicable subscriptions, purchases or payments
  • Provide customer and technical support
  • Resolve disputes and troubleshoot problems
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, misuse or security incidents
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Platform
  • Communicate with you about your account, services, updates or important notices
  • Improve, analyse and develop our services and user experience
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

4. Payments and Payment Information

Where AgriPrep offers paid services, payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

AgriPrep may receive transaction-related information necessary to confirm and manage a payment, such as transaction status, transaction reference and applicable purchase information.

Payment credentials such as card details, banking passwords, UPI PINs or other authentication credentials should be provided only through the authorized payment interface of the applicable payment service provider.

Security Notice: AgriPrep will never ask you to share your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN, banking password or similar payment credentials through email, phone, message or unofficial communication.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share or allow access to information where reasonably necessary to operate AgriPrep and provide its services.

This may include sharing information with:

  • Service providers that support authentication, storage, security or other Platform functions

  • Government authorities or law enforcement agencies where required or permitted by applicable law

Third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms and applicable laws.

6. Legal and Regulatory Disclosures

We may disclose personal information when we believe that such disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, court orders or lawful requests from government or law enforcement authorities.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to enforce our Terms & Conditions, protect our rights or property, investigate fraud or misuse, or protect the safety and security of users, AgriPrep or the general public.

7. Security Precautions

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and should immediately notify us if you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization.

When personal information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable law and legitimate business requirements.
