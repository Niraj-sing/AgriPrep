LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AgriPrep collects, uses, stores and protects information associated with your AgriPrep account and use of the platform.

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AgriPrep collects information that you provide when creating and maintaining your account.

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • College or university
  • Degree or field of study
  • Current year or semester
  • Target examination
  • Account-related information required for authentication

We use this information to create and manage your AgriPrep account and provide account-related features.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information associated with your account may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your AgriPrep account
  • Authenticate your account
  • Verify your email address
  • Provide account-related features
  • Maintain your profile
  • Help keep your account secure
  • Communicate with you regarding your account when necessary

We do not use your account information for purposes unrelated to providing and maintaining AgriPrep unless required by law or you otherwise provide consent.

3. Firebase

AgriPrep uses Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication and account-related functionality.

Your account information may therefore be processed and stored through Firebase services.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms governing their services.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal account information.

Your information may be processed by service providers used to operate AgriPrep where necessary to provide authentication, storage, security and related services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of AgriPrep, our users or others.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your AgriPrep account and associated account information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the support email provided below.

When a deletion request is processed, we will delete or anonymize account information that we are not legally required to retain.

Account deletion & privacy requests support.agriprep@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

AgriPrep is intended for users who are able to use the application in accordance with applicable laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to AgriPrep improperly, please contact us.

8. Third-Party Services

AgriPrep may use third-party services to provide essential application functionality.

Currently, Firebase is used for authentication and related account services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to AgriPrep, our services or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your account information or a data deletion request, please contact AgriPrep Support.

AgriPrep Support support.agriprep@gmail.com
